# Cobblemon: Field Investigations

**An unknown Pokémon has been reported nearby. Find out what it is, track it down, and catch it.**

Field Investigations adds a full detective loop to [Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon): collect evidence in
the field, analyse it in lab mini-games, rule out suspects, then hunt the target with a radar, a map and a scanner.
Everything fits in a single item, the **Field Kit**, with its own pixel-art screens.

The cleaner your investigation, the better the reward: up to **four perfect IVs**, better shiny odds and richer loot.

## How it works

1. **Open a case** with the Field Kit. A trace zone is reported, with a line-up of suspects that really spawn in that biome.
2. **Sample the traces.** Residue, deposits, footprints, nests, claw marks: each has its own sampling mini-game. The
   kit's proximity radar leads you to them.
3. **Dig up buried clues.** Bones, eggshells and fangs are hidden underground. Dig carefully and uncover them with a
   brush. Break the block and the clue is lost.
4. **Analyse your samples** in the pocket sequencer to reveal the genetic markers of the target.
5. **Work the case board.** Compare each suspect with your results and cross out the ones that do not fit. It is your
   deduction: the mod does not do it for you.
6. **Name your suspect** and start the hunt.
7. **Track the target by its cries.** Take bearings from several spots and the kit map gives you a fix.
8. **Scan it from afar** to confirm its identity. Get too close too soon and it runs.
9. **Sneak in and catch it.**

## Features

- **15 mini-games**: memory, timing, steady hand, code breaking, colour mixing, pattern matching and more.
- **Buried clues** to dig up and brush out, like suspicious sand.
- **A 5-star rating** for every case. Failed mini-games, a fleeing target or a wrong guess cost stars; stars decide the
  perfect IVs, the shiny odds and the loot.
- **Data-driven loot**: one loot table per star level, overridable with a data pack.
- **A scanner view** with zoom, like a spyglass.
- **Suspects taken from the real spawn pool** of the biome, so the investigation matches your world.
- **Fully configurable**: distances, number of suspects, cooldowns, difficulty of the hunt, excluded species labels.
- **English and French** included; other languages only need a JSON file.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**, **Fabric**
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- [Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon) **1.8+**

Required on both the client and the server.

## Getting started

Craft a **Field Kit** (redstone, two iron ingots, a compass and a glass pane) and a few **Empty Vials** (three glass
panes), bring a vanilla **brush**, then right-click with the kit.

Legendary, mythical, ultra beast and paradox Pokémon are excluded by default.
